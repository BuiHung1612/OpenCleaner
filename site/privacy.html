Privacy Policy

OpenCleaner for macOS and Windows · last updated 20 November 2026

What OpenCleaner collects

Nothing. OpenCleaner has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads. It scans and cleans your disk on your computer, and nothing it finds (file names, sizes, apps) leaves your computer.

What stays on your computer

  • Your settings, and a history of what was cleaned (so items can be put back from the Trash or Recycle Bin).
  • Caches of scan results, to show pages faster.

Uninstalling OpenCleaner and removing its folder (macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/OpenCleaner; Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenCleaner) removes them.

Network

  • Update check: OpenCleaner asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest release, and downloads it from GitHub when you choose to install it. You can turn this off in Settings. The Microsoft Store version leaves updates to the Store and does not check.
  • Crash reports: after a crash OpenCleaner offers to open a GitHub issue in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you review it and post it yourself.

GitHub's own privacy statement applies to those requests.
